Current Requires a Source

A base model learns from a bounded training snapshot, while the world keeps changing. A product may add browsing, retrieval, tools, stored memory, or newer instructions. Fluent wording alone does not reveal which information source, date, or product layer shaped an answer.

Original source — 2021
"A peer-reviewed study published in October 2021 found that the three most-visited websites globally were Google, YouTube, and Facebook, in that order, based on monthly active user data compiled by a web analytics firm."
Specific. Dated. Sourced. Scoped.

Rephrased in a blog post — 2022

The date is gone. "Recent" now does the work the date used to do.
2
Compressed into a listicle — 2023
"The top websites on the internet are Google, YouTube, and Facebook."
Source gone. Qualifier gone. A present-tense assertion that reads as permanently true.

Absorbed into training data
[A base model could encounter many differently dated and sourced versions of the claim during training. Its learned parameters do not function as a citation ledger for each generated phrase.]
Training can preserve a linguistic pattern without preserving a usable source trail.

Training boundary versus product access

Every trained model version has a bounded training period, but there may not be one simple public “cutoff” for every data source. At use time, a product can also provide retrieved documents, browsing results, conversation memory, tools, or system instructions. Ask what information was available for this specific answer rather than treating the base model and the product as the same thing.

Key line "Currency belongs to the answer's source chain, not its tone. Ask what information was available, when it was dated, and how you can verify it."
Base model and product are different evidence layers. Browsing or retrieval can supply current material; it can also return stale, low-quality, or misread sources. A safety layer may flag uncertainty; another interface may not. None of those behaviors settles the philosophical question of understanding. For current claims, inspect dates, sources, retrieval behavior, and corroboration.
